My Path

My career started on the production floor, cutting aerospace parts to blueprint tolerances. From there I moved into software development at RIT, then into SQL reporting and vehicle software quality at BMW Group. The common thread is finding what is actually going wrong, backing it up with evidence, and explaining it so people can act on it. I'm now focusing on data analytics while staying open to quality and validation roles.
