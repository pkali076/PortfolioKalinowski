MN Cockpit

SQL | Oracle APEX | KPI Reporting | Power BI report migration

A one-page quality KPI dashboard for the MN (procurement) department at BMW Group's Spartanburg Plant, which focuses on the quality of parts and vehicles as they are produced. I wrote the SQL, built the dashboard in Oracle APEX, and moved existing Power BI report logic into it so managers could see repair minutes per unit, top defect drivers, and open defects in one place.
